# Channel notes: atarikafa, ZindanCraft season

What is specific to this channel and this series. The method is in `retention-edit`; this guide only changes its details.

## The channel

- Turkish Minecraft long-form. No face camera. The voice, the captions and the counters do the work a face would.
- Because there is no face, never leave a silent stretch, and keep every spoken reaction ("oha", laughter, shouting). The reaction is in the sound.
- Game sound stays audible under the voice: the mod's boss music and voice lines are half the scene.
- The mod's text is English. The Turkish caption must be the largest text on screen whenever an English banner or menu is visible.
- All on-screen text is Turkish and upper case. Title cards three words at most.

## The series

ZindanCraft is the "Atarikafa Dungeons" mod: pick a class, go down a dungeon, beat the boss, take its key to the guild, unlock the next tier. Tier 1 to Tier 5, then the crown, then the Abyss. Each episode makes one promise and ends on one result, 15 to 25 minutes. Episode 1 is the pilot: from nothing to the first Tier 1 boss and key #1.

## Moments the mod gives for free

Look for these in the inventory first. They replace effects other channels build by hand.

| Moment | Use |
|---|---|
| Boss wakes, "X is enraged!", ring of fire | Promise shot or last teaser shot |
| Soul stone rises where the player died, coordinates in chat | Teaser shot; in the body, keep the death and the walk back short but keep both |
| Dungeon note: "Cleared · Grade S · 2 deaths" | Final scene, held 3 seconds; also a teaser shot |
| Entry banner: "The Hollow Crypt · Tier 1 · Level 1–5" | Chapter card. Hold it about 2 seconds with a Turkish caption |
| Item tooltip ("every 5th hit calls lightning") | Hold 2 seconds, then cut to the first hit |
| Class choice screen, skill tree | Progress beat, like a recipe-unlocked popup |
| Ship, cannon, pirate island | Scene change when the dungeon footage runs long |

## Counter

The counter for this series is the tier and the floor: `TIER 1`, `KAT 2 / 6`, later the Abyss depth. Keep it in a top corner whenever the player is in a dungeon and change it at every floor.

## Cut on sight

- JEI panel, minimap box or English menu clutter filling the screen with nothing said.
- Long setup talk at the start.
- Walking between places with no talk.
